GOOD QUESTIONS / 08

Before we begin.

No fog, no mysterious process. Here are the practical details.

Can we start with one small problem?

Yes. A focused first phase is often the strongest way to prove value, reduce risk and learn what the larger system should become.

Are the prices fixed?

No. They are indicative starting prices in INR. Scope, integrations, timeline, taxes and third-party costs are confirmed in a written proposal.

Will AI replace our team?

The goal is to remove repetitive effort and improve decisions. Human review stays wherever context, judgment or accountability matters.

What do we own after launch?

Your data and agreed deliverables are handled according to the project agreement. Third-party software, licenses and pre-existing components retain their own terms.

What happens after handover?

Each project includes an agreed bug-fix period. Ongoing support, new features and maintenance can be scoped separately.
