PRICING FAQ / 04

Everything upfront.

Are these fixed prices?+

No. These are indicative starting prices in INR, not binding quotations. Final scope, integrations, timelines and taxes are confirmed in a written proposal.

Are hosting and third-party tools included?+

Unless explicitly quoted, domain, hosting, AI API usage, WhatsApp/SMS fees and other third-party subscriptions are separate.

Can I start small and upgrade later?+

Yes. We can define a small first phase, validate it and plan later improvements separately.

How do payments work?+

A milestone-based payment schedule is agreed before work begins. Typical projects can use an advance, development milestone and handover payment.

Who owns the website and data?+

Your data and agreed deliverables are handled according to the project agreement. Third-party licenses and pre-existing components may have separate terms.

What happens after launch?+

An agreed bug-fix period is included in each scoped package; ongoing support, changes and maintenance are quoted separately.
